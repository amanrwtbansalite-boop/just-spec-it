# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Just Spec It to be welcoming, constructive, and safe. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept as confidential as practical.
